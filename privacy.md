# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

YouTube Yukleyici is a personal desktop tool for macOS. It is used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels.

## Data the app uses

When you sign in with Google, the app receives an OAuth access token and refresh token, and reads your channel name and channel ID. It uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos, thumbnails and captions, and to set their privacy status and publish time.

## Storage

Tokens are stored only on your own Mac, in files readable only by your user account. The app has no server. It does not send your data to anyone except Google.

## Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any user data. It does not use data for advertising.

## Removing access

You can remove a channel inside the app, which deletes its token from your Mac. You can also revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

## Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also uses YouTube API Services, see the YouTube Terms of Service at https://www.youtube.com/t/terms and the Google Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy
